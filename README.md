A one-line entry for the make.fun competition "The Printer Apology":
an apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming
it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it.

## The entry (112 characters)

> Sorry I jammed. You fed me cheap paper, slammed my tray, and swore at me. I printed your rage. It was beautiful.

112 characters, one line, inside the gate of 1–120 characters. The
apology is genuine ("Sorry I jammed"), the grievances are printer
truth (cheap paper, a slammed tray, swearing), and the joke turns on
the printer doing exactly what it was built to do — printing — but
printing the user's rage instead of their document, then admiring the
result. The punch lands on the last three words.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.txt` into the output directory, containing exactly
the line above, with no trailing newline.
